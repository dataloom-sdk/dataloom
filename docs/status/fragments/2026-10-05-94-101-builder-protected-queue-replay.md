# Fragment: `#94` + `#101` -- DataLoomBuilder queue workers replay through provider protection (2026-10-05)

For the lead to fold into `docs/status/market-readiness.md`. Not edited by this PR.

## (a) Proposed "Recently shipped" row

| 2026-10-05 | `#94`/`#101`: `DataLoomBuilder`'s queue workers (`queueWorkerConfiguration` and `circuitQueueWorkerConfiguration`) now replay queued entries through `ProviderProtectedQueuedSynchronizationExecutionHandler` -- the same transport/storage circuit breaker and retry mapping as `DataLoom.protectedSynchronization` -- whenever `providerProtectionConfiguration` is also configured. Before this the handler existed and was unit-tested but was wired nowhere, and both builder workers replayed through the unprotected coordinator, so a transport circuit could not be opened or observed through a builder-assembled worker (finding from the `#101` retry PR #483). Opt-in: with no `providerProtectionConfiguration` the historical handler is used unchanged (control test asserts this). Private change inside `DataLoomBuilder` only; no public surface or ABI change (`updateKotlinAbi` produced no diff; `checkKotlinAbi` green in both normal and `DATALOOM_ANDROID_BUILD=true` configurations). Proof through a real `DataLoomBuilder`: `DataLoomBuilderProtectedQueueReplayTest` (runtime `commonTest`, JVM run, real wall-clock time via `runBlocking`, 3 tests: direct worker, circuit-aware worker, unprotected control): first replay fails and is rescheduled with `retryAttempt` advanced; the second failure opens the circuit; a later queued entry in the same cycle is rejected with `PROVIDER_CIRCUIT_OPEN` BEFORE the transport (pull count stays 2) and is rescheduled, not failed; a cycle inside the open window is rejected again; after the real open duration the half-open probe reaches the transport, both entries complete. `ProtectedQueueRobolectricTest` repeats the scenario over the real Room queue and `RoomCircuitBreakerStateStore` (circuit phase read back from a fresh Room connection: OPEN, then CLOSED). Revert-and-observe: forcing the builder back to the unprotected handler fails both direct/circuit-worker runtime tests and the Android test at the cycle-2 reschedule count (e2 reaches the transport and completes instead of being gated). Full `:dataloom-runtime:jvmTest`: 2157 tests, 0 failures. **Not proven:** Apple counterpart (the builder change is common code, so it applies to iOS, but no iOS reference-consumer test drives it), an emulator run, a real WorkManager tick or process kill between attempts, durably admitted strategy plans under protection, and per-entry provider evidence surfaced through the builder worker's result types. | `#94`/`#101` platform |
